Managed Brokerage Workflow

We keep the process organized from lead to closing.

The platform is designed around privacy, verification and managed matching—not direct public sharing of owner or buyer contact details.

📥

1. Lead capture

Sellers upload property information and buyers submit their requirements through dedicated forms.

🛡️

2. Verification

Your team checks basic details, documents and intent before moving the lead forward.

🧠

3. Matching

Buyer budgets, locations and property preferences are compared against suitable seller inventory.

📞

4. Team contact

Authorized staff handles calls and keeps both sides from directly bypassing the workflow.

📍

5. Site visit

Visits are scheduled by the company team with notes and follow-up status tracked internally.

✅

6. Deal coordination

Negotiation, documentation assistance and closing follow-ups can be managed as paid services.

Privacy architecture

Public listing ≠ public personal data.

The public site should show property type, area, approximate locality, price and curated photos only. Phone numbers and ownership documents belong in a private backend accessible only to authorized staff.

Operations ready

Built for a sales team.

In production, every lead can have an ID, assigned executive, follow-up date, matching score, visit status and deal stage.
